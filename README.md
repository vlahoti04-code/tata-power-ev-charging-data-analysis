# Tata Power EV Charging Data Analysis

## Project Overview

The **Tata Power EV Charging Data Analysis** project analyzes EV
charging-network performance using operational, customer, demand, and
financial metrics.

The project is designed to answer three major business questions:

1.  **Performance** -- How effectively are charging stations and
    infrastructure being utilized?
2.  **Profitability** -- What do revenue, ROI, EBITDA-oriented measures,
    and payback indicate about project economics?
3.  **Expansion** -- Which districts and site types should receive
    attention when planning future charging infrastructure?

The analysis brings these dimensions together to support data-driven
decisions around station performance, infrastructure allocation,
customer demand, and future expansion.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to combine charging-network usage,
customer behaviour, and financial ROI indicators to identify operational
gaps and expansion opportunities.

The project focuses on:

-   Understanding charging demand across districts.
-   Evaluating station and plug utilization.
-   Studying charging-session and energy-consumption patterns.
-   Understanding customer and vehicle-related demand patterns.
-   Comparing financial performance using revenue, ROI, and payback
    metrics.
-   Identifying areas where infrastructure capacity may not match actual
    demand.
-   Supporting future charging-station expansion decisions.

## Analysis Areas

### 1. Network Performance

The network-performance analysis looks at:

-   Total revenue
-   Energy consumed
-   Charging sessions
-   Active customers
-   Day-wise usage
-   Hourly demand
-   Monthly demand
-   District-wise demand

The report highlights **Jayanagar and Rajajinagar** as major demand
centres. It also reports noticeable usage drops on **Fridays and
Saturdays**, indicating an opportunity for demand-generation initiatives
during lower-demand periods.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 2. Station Utilization & Infrastructure

The operational analysis focuses on whether available charging
infrastructure is sufficient for observed demand.

Key measures include:

-   Station utilization
-   Plug utilization
-   Actual charging demand
-   Projected plug demand
-   District-wise load
-   Energy consumed per station

The report identifies a significant infrastructure deficit in
**Jayanagar and Rajajinagar**, where actual charging volume is reported
to exceed projected plug capacity.

This indicates that infrastructure allocation can be reviewed in
high-demand districts.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 3. Customer & Charging Behaviour

The customer analysis examines:

-   Active customers
-   Charging sessions
-   Sessions per customer
-   Energy consumption
-   Vehicle/battery capacity patterns
-   Revenue across income segments

The report highlights higher-capacity vehicle groups as important
contributors to charging-energy demand and shows revenue distribution
across different income segments.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 4. Financial Performance

The financial analysis evaluates the economics of charging
infrastructure using:

-   Revenue
-   CapEx
-   ROI
-   EBITDA-oriented measures
-   Payback period
-   Project ranking
-   Site-type performance

The dashboard reports:

-   **Portfolio ROI: 41.43%**
-   **Average Payback: 5 years**

The report also states that **Mall/Retail** installations have the
longest average payback at approximately **5.18 years**, while
**Residential Complex** installations are highlighted with a shorter
reported payback in the strategic recommendations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Key Business Insights

### Demand Concentration

Jayanagar and Rajajinagar are identified as major demand centres. The
report indicates that these areas experience higher charging demand
while facing an infrastructure-capacity gap.

### Low-Demand Periods

The analysis reports noticeable usage reductions on Fridays and
Saturdays. This creates a potential opportunity to use promotional or
time-based pricing strategies to improve utilization during lower-demand
periods.

### Infrastructure Allocation

The project recommends reviewing infrastructure allocation toward
districts where actual demand is higher than available/projected plug
capacity.

### Site Economics

The report compares site types using payback and ROI-oriented measures.
Residential Complex installations are highlighted as having a shorter
reported payback, while Mall/Retail sites are reported with a longer
average payback.

### Expansion Planning

Future expansion can be evaluated using a combination of:

-   Demand
-   Station utilization
-   District-level capacity gaps
-   ROI
-   Payback
-   Site type

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Strategic Recommendations

Based on the supplied project report:

1.  **Strengthen infrastructure in high-demand districts** such as
    Jayanagar and Rajajinagar.
2.  **Review future site expansion using demand and financial metrics
    together.**
3.  **Consider demand-generation strategies** for lower-utilization
    periods, particularly Friday and Saturday.
4.  **Evaluate residential and other site types using payback and ROI
    before expansion.**
5.  **Prioritize locations where charging demand and infrastructure
    capacity show a significant mismatch.**
6.  **Use dashboard KPIs for continuous monitoring of network
    performance and financial health.**
